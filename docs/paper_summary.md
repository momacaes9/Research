# Study summary

## Background
- Bell pepper is among the vegetables with the highest pesticide residue in Brazil and is economically important, especially in São Paulo state.
- Synthetic NPK fertilizers raise yields but bring leaching, CO2 emissions and ammonia volatilization. Nature-Based Solutions (NBS) look for plant-derived alternatives.
- Neem (*Azadirachta indica*, Meliaceae) leaves contain N, P, K, Ca, Mg, proteins (~7.1%), carbohydrates (~22.9%), amino acids, flavonoids and limonoids (azadirachtin, nimbin, salannin).

## Hypothesis
Nutrients in Neem leaf extract help bell pepper seedlings grow faster than water alone.

## Methods
1. **Drying**: 50 g commercial Neem leaves dried in an oven (3 cycles) until constant mass; total mass loss 15.28% (50.0 → 42.36 g).
2. **Extraction**: dried leaves blended 4 min with 200 mL PA ethanol (99 °GL), sieved, filtered with lab filter paper, dried at 38 °C. Yield: 0.1977 g solid extract.
3. **Formulation**: extract dissolved in 50 mL water, brought to 200 mL. Control = water.
4. **Application**: 1 mL at the root of each seedling, with daily watering (except Sundays and holidays).
5. **Measurement**: stem height (cm) once a week for 10 weeks.
6. **Chemistry (IQ-USP)**: thin-layer chromatography on silica gel, methanol : ethyl acetate 1:2, read at 254 nm and 365 nm.

## Chemistry result (TLC)
Multiple spots, mostly pink fluorescence at 365 nm, consistent with flavonoids (absorb ~350–400 nm). The Neem extract is chemically more complex than a single-compound extract.

## Earlier phase (2024, preliminary)
A 4-week pilot with 4 groups (control, Neem, clove, Neem + clove; 2 seedlings each) suggested Neem increased stem height and leaf count, and clove extract reduced aphids (*Icerya purchasi*). In the 2025 repeat, clove groups failed because the clove solution became over-concentrated; only the Neem vs control comparison is analysed here.

## Scalability note (from the paper)
Global vegetable area (~131 M acres) would need ~1.3 Mt of neem inputs a year; effective agricultural neem supply is ~0.77 Mt, so near-term use should target high-value organic crops or neem–synthetic hybrid systems.
